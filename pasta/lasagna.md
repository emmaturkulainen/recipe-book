# World's Best Lasagna
## Ingredients
### Ragu

400g beef mince (15-20% fat)\
100g pancetta or bacon\
1 medium onion\
1 carrot\
3-6 garlic cloves\
1 tbsp olive oil\
1 tbsp tomato paste\
150ml dry red wine\
400g canned whole peeled tomatoes\
150ml whole milk\
140ml beef stock\
1 tsp Worcestershire sauce\
oregano\
nutmeg\
salt\
black pepper

### Bechamel

50g butter\
50g flour\
600ml whole milk\
50g parmesan cheese\
nutmeg\
salt\
white or black pepper

## Make the ragu
Fry pancetta in a heavy pot over medium heat until browned.\
Add oil, onion, carrot, and a pinch of salt. Cook 10–15 min on medium-low until soft and sweet.\
Add garlic, cook 30–60 sec.\
Turn heat up. Add beef and let it brown properly before stirring.\
Add tomato paste, cook 2–3 min until dark red.\
Add wine, scrape the bottom, and reduce until almost gone.\
Add crushed tomatoes, stock, Worcestershire, oregano and pepper.\
Simmer gently, partly covered, for 1½–2½ hours until thick. Add water if it gets dry.\
In the last 30 min, stir in the milk and nutmeg.

## Make the bechamel
Melt butter over medium-low heat. Whisk in flour for 2 min without browning.\
Slowly whisk in milk. Cook 5–8 min, stirring, until it coats a spoon.\
Take off the heat. Stir in Parmesan, nutmeg, salt and pepper. It should be pourable.

## Assembly

Butter the dish.\
Layer pasta, ragu and bechamel. Repeat.\
Finish with béchamel, mozzarella and Parmesan.\
Bake at about 200°C. Cover for first 20–25 min. Cook uncovered for another 20-25 min.\
Rest 10–15 min before cutting.
