# World's Best Lasagna
## Ingredients
### Ragu

400g beef mince (15-20% fat)\
100g pancetta or bacon\
1 medium onion\
1 carrot\
3-6 garlic cloves\
1 tbsp olive oil\
1 tbsp tomato paste\
150ml dry red wine\
400g canned whole peeled tomatoes\
150ml whole milk\
140ml beef stock\
1 tsp Worcestershire sauce\
oregano\
nutmeg\
salt\
black pepper

### Bechamel

50g butter\
50g flour\
600ml whole milk\
50g parmesan cheese\
nutmeg\
salt\
white or black pepper
